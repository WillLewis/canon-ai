Terms & Trust — Canon AI

DRAFT — FOR LEGAL REVIEW. This page states the product's commitments in plain English. It is not yet a reviewed legal document.

1 · Your IP stays yours DRAFT — FOR LEGAL REVIEW

Everything you upload — scripts, world documents, notes — and everything Canon derives from it (assertions, findings, reports, bibles) is your material. You grant Canon a license only to the extent needed to operate the service for you: storing your material, indexing it, running checks over it, and showing it back to you and the collaborators you invite. No other license. No right to sublicense, publish, or reuse your material for any other purpose.

2 · No-training warranty DRAFT — FOR LEGAL REVIEW

Canon never trains machine-learning models on your material, and never permits anyone else to. The AI extraction step calls the Anthropic API in a no-training configuration: your scripts are processed to index facts you wrote, and are not used to train the models that process them. Canon holds this line contractually with its providers and operationally in its own code.

3 · Export anytime, delete means deleted DRAFT — FOR LEGAL REVIEW

You can export everything at any time — every stored row, your rulings and their attribution, the rendered bible and report — in open formats (JSON Lines and Markdown). Deleting a world or your account removes your content fully from the live product: rows are hard-deleted, not hidden or flagged. Transient copies in short-lived infrastructure backups expire on our provider's standard schedule and are never restored into the product; we state this plainly rather than over-promise instant erasure of every backup medium.

4 · Canon never writes your story DRAFT — FOR LEGAL REVIEW

Canon produces no literary material — no loglines, no dialogue, no pitches, no beats, no synopses. It indexes, retrieves, checks, and explains what you wrote, and every flag or answer cites the scene that establishes it. The writer is the only generator. A feature that would require generating story content is out of scope of this product, by design and by policy.

Questions about any of the above: the export and delete controls live on each world's page and on your account page — they are product features, not a support ticket.
